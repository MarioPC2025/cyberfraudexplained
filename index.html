Real scams, taken apart.

Cyber Fraud Explained is an independent educational YouTube channel. Each episode breaks down a real fraud case, from business email compromise and fake invoices to AI voice-cloning scams, shows the red flags, and explains how to defend against it.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@cyberfraudexplained

Publishing pipeline

The channel is produced with a private, in-house publishing tool, the Cyber Fraud Explained Publishing Pipeline. It is used only by the channel owner to upload finished episodes to the channel's own YouTube account. It uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the finished video, always as private, for a final manual review in YouTube Studio;
  • set the video's title, description, tags and custom thumbnail;
  • upload the episode's subtitle files.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's YouTube account or data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
